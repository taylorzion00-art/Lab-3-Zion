The Best Movie I've Seen Lately: Spider-Man: Brand New Day

A movie review by Zion

Why I Picked This Movie

Honestly the best movie I've seen in a while is Spider-Man: Brand New Day. I went to see it in theatres this summer and I had pretty high expectations going in because No Way Home ended on such a sad note. Like Peter literally lost everybody at the end of that one, so I really wanted to see where they would take him next. Tom Holland is still Spider-Man, and Zendaya and Jacob Batalon are back as MJ and Ned, which I was really happy about.

What the Story Is About

From what I remember, the movie starts a few years after No Way Home and nobody in the world knows who Peter Parker is anymore. He's basically just Spider-Man all the time now, fighting crime around New York by himself with nobody to go home to. The part that got me was seeing MJ and Ned living their lives and being happy without him, because he knows them but they have no idea who he is. Then all that stress and being alone starts doing something to him, and his powers start changing in a way he can't really control. I don't want to spoil too much but it gets pretty intense.

What I Liked Most

I think my favourite part was how the movie actually took its time with Peter being lonely instead of just jumping from fight to fight. There were scenes where I actually felt bad for him. The action was still really good though, and I loved seeing the Punisher show up because Jon Bernthal was perfect for that role. Mark Ruffalo coming back as Bruce Banner was a nice surprise too. Overall I would 100% recommend it, especially if you grew up watching Tom Holland's Spider-Man movies like I did.
